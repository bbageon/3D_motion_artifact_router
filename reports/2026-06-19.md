## AR-051 — Cross-generator refinement effect validation

### Scope

Dashboard row: AR-051.

Goal: representative-300 prompt pool에서 MotionGPT / MDM / MoMask output에 동일 fixed refinement tools를 적용하고, 보정 전후의 physical/proxy delta, Category-A standard metric, visual 사례를 분리해 확인한다.

Claim boundary: learned policy 우월성 증명이 아니다. 이 작업은 tool effect와 side-effect를 측정해, 이후 quality-aware ranker / q_proxy 설계 근거를 제공한다.

### Artifacts

- Physical/proxy snapshot: `evals/snapshots/representative_refinement_effect_v1.json`
- Category-A snapshot: `evals/snapshots/standard_metric_representative_refinement_v1.json`
- Visual manifest: `reports/figures/2026-06-19/ar051_refinement_visual_pack/manifest.json`
- Tools:
  - `tools/representative_refinement_effect.py`
  - `tools/standard_metric_representative_refinement.py`
  - `tools/representative_refinement_visual_pack.py`

### Protocol

- Dataset: representative-300 prompt bank, 3 seeds per prompt, 3 generators.
- Statistical unit: prompt. Seed-level outputs are averaged within prompt before aggregate reporting.
- MotionGPT sensitivity: `actual_generated / target_length < 0.6` samples excluded in sensitivity view.
- Tool variants tested in physical/proxy pass:
  - STOP/noop
  - FootLock small/medium/large
  - VelocitySmoothing small/medium/large
  - BoneProjection all-chains small/medium/large
- Category-A pass:
  - original
  - FootLock large
  - VelocitySmoothing medium
  - BoneProjection all-chains large

### Main Results

#### 1. Fixed FootLock is not a safe generic solution

FootLock large reduced artifact proxy but increased world foot-skate across all generators.

| Generator | Δfoot_skate_world | Δartifact_total | ΔFID | ΔR@1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| MotionGPT | +0.016364 | -0.006796 | +0.056 | -0.005 |
| MDM | +0.015558 | -0.008001 | +0.409 | -0.002 |
| MoMask | +0.015205 | -0.000917 | +0.061 | +0.002 |

Interpretation: artifact_total만 보면 개선처럼 보이지만, world-space foot-skate와 FID에서는 악화가 나타난다. 이 결과는 "artifact 감소만으로 motion quality 증가를 주장하면 안 된다"는 기존 논의와 일치한다.

#### 2. VelocitySmoothing gives small physical gains but weak standard-quality gains

VelocitySmoothing medium reduced foot-skate / acceleration, but standard metric improvement was limited and semantic alignment was not consistently preserved.

| Generator | Δfoot_skate_world | Δaccel | Δartifact_total | ΔFID | ΔR@1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| MotionGPT | -0.001195 | -0.003063 | -0.000260 | +0.014 | -0.004 |
| MDM | -0.000762 | -0.001249 | +0.000459 | -0.057 | -0.007 |
| MoMask | -0.000694 | -0.002169 | +0.000931 | +0.009 | -0.000 |

Interpretation: smoothing은 일부 physical magnitude를 줄이지만, 그 자체로 quality improvement policy는 아니다. MDM에서 FID는 조금 좋아졌지만 R@1은 하락했다.

#### 3. BoneProjection is mostly quality-preserving, but not uniformly improving

BoneProjection all-chains large reduced artifact_total strongly for MotionGPT/MDM and preserved Category-A for MotionGPT/MoMask, but MDM has fidelity/side-effect cost.

| Generator | Δfoot_skate_world | Δartifact_total | MPJPE | ΔFID | ΔR@1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| MotionGPT | -0.000717 | -0.011454 | 0.006972 | +0.001 | +0.001 |
| MDM | -0.001144 | -0.010793 | 0.024062 | +0.060 | -0.002 |
| MoMask | +0.000347 | -0.006550 | 0.005703 | -0.000 | -0.001 |

Interpretation: BoneProjection은 좋은 후보지만 generator별 side-effect가 다르다. 특히 MDM에서는 MPJPE가 커서 "artifact는 줄지만 원본 동작을 많이 바꾸는" 위험이 있다.

  - prompt: "the person is running around in a circle."

GIF sanity: both GIFs nonblank, Y-up renderer, side-by-side before/after.

### Research Interpretation

Judgment:

Fixed tools have real effects, but their effects are not aligned enough to serve as a final policy. The result supports moving from "artifact-tool matching" to "effect-aware quality-gain ranking."

Evidence grounding:

- HumanML3D / T2M evaluator: Category-A FID/R-Precision/MM-Dist evaluation context.
- PhysDiff motivation: physical/contact plausibility is necessary because generative outputs can violate contact/physics-like constraints.
- MoMask context: masked-token generator comparison shows generator family affects error profile.

Application scope:

This is real-distribution evidence on representative prompts, but still fixed-tool diagnostic. It does not prove learned policy improvement.

Uncertainty:

- Category-A metrics are aggregate distribution metrics, not per-sample perceptual labels.
- Visual pack has only two qualitative cases.
- Tool target is still coarse; target-aware tools may outperform fixed full-body/both-feet application.

### Intent-Reconciliation

1. User intent: 실제 generator output에서 문제가 있고, tool 적용이 실제 quality 개선으로 이어지는지 확인.
2. Implementation: representative-300 × 3 generator fixed-tool before/after evaluation + standard metrics + visual examples.
3. Result alignment: aligned.
4. Claim scope: fixed-tool diagnostic only; learned policy claim not made.
5. Side effects: FootLock failure preserved as negative result; no pool mutation.

Verdict: aligned.

## AR-040 — v0 q_proxy + M1 dense effect oracle

### Scope

Dashboard row: AR-040.

Goal: AR-051에서 확인한 fixed-tool trade-off를 v0 ranker 학습용 label로 바꾼다. 이 작업은 learned policy 성능 증명이 아니라, `q_proxy_v0.1`과 M1 dense effect oracle을 고정하는 준비 단계다.

### Artifacts

- Spec: `.claude/docs/dashboard-task-specs/AR-040-v0-quality-aware-ranker.md`
- Tool: `tools/effect_aware_v0_oracle.py`
- Snapshot: `evals/snapshots/effect_aware_v0_oracle_v1.json`

### q_proxy Definition

`q_proxy_v0.1`은 다음을 보상한다.

- artifact_total 감소
- world foot-skate 감소
- acceleration 감소
- FootFloating fire 감소

다음은 감점한다.

- fidelity MPJPE 증가
- correction magnitude 증가
- 새 physical gate fire 발생
- foot-skate 악화

FID/R-Precision/MM-Dist는 최종 validation 지표이므로 직접 label에 넣지 않았다. Semantic block은 v0에서 neutral-impute(`semantic_confidence=0`)로 두었고, semantic-aware ranking claim은 하지 않는다.

### Dataset

- states: 2,700 = representative-300 × 3 seed × 3 generator
- transitions: 27,000 = states × 10 candidate actions
- oracle rows: 900 = 300 prompt × 3 generator
- state: v0 40-dim before-action observable vector
- action: `(tool, u)` 10-dim encoded vector

### M1 Dense Oracle Results

| Generator | non-STOP rate | mean best q_proxy | mean gain over STOP | Main selected actions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| MotionGPT | 99.7% | +0.497 | +0.497 | VelocitySmoothing medium 148, small 47, large 37 |
| MDM | 83.0% | +0.154 | +0.154 | VelocitySmoothing small 84, medium 73, STOP 51 |
| MoMask | 96.3% | +0.275 | +0.275 | VelocitySmoothing medium 154, small 86 |

Interpretation: v0 oracle is mostly selecting smoothing actions. This means `q_proxy_v0.1` now identifies some quality-gain candidates, but it is not yet STOP-calibrated. The high non-STOP rate, especially MotionGPT/MoMask, should not be treated as final policy behavior.

### Failure Check

AR-051에서 중요했던 FootLock failure가 `q_proxy`에서 제대로 낮게 평가되는지 확인했다.

| Generator | mean artifact gain | mean foot-skate gain | mean q_proxy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| MotionGPT | +0.0068 | -0.0164 | -3.351 |
| MDM | +0.0080 | -0.0156 | -3.400 |
| MoMask | +0.0009 | -0.0152 | -3.471 |

Interpretation: artifact는 조금 좋아져도 foot-skate가 악화되는 action을 강하게 벌점 처리한다. 따라서 AR-051의 핵심 failure mode가 q_proxy에 반영됐다.

### Honest Limitations

- `q_proxy_v0.1`은 internal proxy assumption이다.
- STOP threshold / minimum improvement threshold가 아직 없다. M1 oracle은 "조금이라도 q_proxy가 높으면 act"하는 경향이 있다.
- v0 state는 target을 보지 않는다. target-aware improvement는 AR-041에서 검증해야 한다.
- semantic scalar는 아직 실제 tm2t per-sample precompute가 아니라 neutral-impute다.

### Intent-Reconciliation

1. User intent: artifact-tool matching이 아니라 quality gain을 예측하는 정책으로 전환.
2. Implementation: AR-051 effect를 바탕으로 q_proxy를 정의하고, v0 state/action-effect oracle을 생성.
3. Result alignment: aligned.
4. Claim scope: learned policy 성능 주장 없음.
5. Side effects: full transition raw는 compact snapshot으로 요약; deterministic 재생성 가능.

Verdict: aligned.

### Next Dashboard Item

AR-040 다음은 Dashboard 기준으로 AR-041 또는 새 row가 필요하다. 연구 흐름상 바로 학습으로 가려면 별도 row로 "v0 learned ranker train/eval"을 backlog에 등록하는 것이 자연스럽다. AR-041은 target-aware 확장이므로, v0 learned baseline을 먼저 닫는 편이 더 안전하다.

## AR-052 — v0 learned ranker train/eval

### Scope

Dashboard row: AR-052.

Goal: AR-040의 M1 dense effect oracle을 learned v0 ranker가 얼마나 근사하는지 확인한다. 이 작업은 q_proxy 기준 offline ranking 평가이며, final Category-A quality claim은 하지 않는다.

### Artifacts

- Tool: `tools/effect_aware_v0_learned_ranker.py`
- Snapshot: `evals/snapshots/effect_aware_v0_learned_ranker_v1.json`

### Protocol

- Dataset: representative-300 × 3 generator × 3 seed × 10 candidate actions = 27,000 rows.
- Split unit: prompt/sample_id.
- Train/test: 210 / 90 prompts.
- Leakage audit: train/test prompt overlap = 0.
- Models: RandomForestRegressor, HistGradientBoostingRegressor.
- Features: v0 state 40 + action 10.
- Label: `q_proxy_v0.1`.

### Main Results

RandomForest was the stronger default in ranking metrics.

| Scope | R2 | MAE | top1 | top3 | regret | random regret | false-improve | act-rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| pooled | 0.788 | 0.413 | 0.489 | 0.811 | 0.116 | 0.928 | 0.170 | 0.956 |
| MotionGPT | 0.762 | 0.460 | 0.433 | 0.800 | 0.198 | 1.089 | 0.156 | 1.000 |
| MDM | 0.836 | 0.365 | 0.378 | 0.822 | 0.062 | 0.778 | 0.178 | 0.789 |
| MoMask | 0.761 | 0.406 | 0.656 | 0.933 | 0.078 | 0.915 | 0.044 | 0.956 |

Interpretation:

1. v0 learned ranker is meaningful: regret is far below random across all scopes.
2. v0 does not fully recover M1 oracle: top1 match is only 49% pooled.
3. top3 is much better than top1: learned ranker often finds the right neighborhood but misses exact strength/tool.
4. false-improve remains non-trivial, especially pooled/MDM/MotionGPT, so direct deployment is not justified.

### STOP Calibration

Pooled RF threshold sweep:

| threshold | act-rate | regret | false-improve |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.00 | 0.956 | 0.116 | 0.170 |
| 0.05 | 0.752 | 0.120 | 0.100 |
| 0.10 | 0.615 | 0.135 | 0.085 |
| 0.20 | 0.363 | 0.174 | 0.056 |
| 0.30 | 0.211 | 0.214 | 0.037 |
| 0.50 | 0.070 | 0.283 | 0.022 |

Interpretation: thresholding can reduce false-improve, but it trades away many interventions and increases regret. This is exactly the calibration problem that should be solved before any closed-loop claim.

### Honest Limitations

- v0 is global-only; it cannot see target/local/relation state.
- semantic features are still neutral-imputed.
- Ranking is q_proxy-only, not Category-A or perceptual validation.
- top1 action precision is not enough for final policy claims.

### Intent-Reconciliation

1. User intent: learned policy가 실제로 oracle을 따라잡는지 확인.
2. Implementation: prompt-level leakage-free split + RF/HGB q_proxy ranking evaluation.
3. Result alignment: aligned.
4. Claim scope: learned v0 ranker is useful but incomplete.
5. Side effects: no model artifact committed; deterministic retraining via tool.

Verdict: aligned.

### Next Dashboard Item

AR-052 결과상 바로 AR-041 target-aware ranker로 넘어갈 근거가 있다. 이유는 v0가 top3 neighborhood는 잡지만 top1 exact action을 자주 놓치기 때문이다. 즉 global state만으로는 action 선택 정밀도가 부족하며, local/relation target state가 필요하다.
